HERE WeGo is a free navigation app developed by HERE Technologies, headquartered in Amsterdam, the Netherlands. Originally developed as Nokia Maps, the company is now owned by a consortium of European automotive manufacturers including BMW, Audi, and Mercedes-Benz. HERE WeGo offers turn-by-turn navigation, public transit routing, and offline maps with a clean, intuitive interface.

## Key Features

- Turn-by-turn voice navigation for driving, walking, and cycling
- Comprehensive public transit routing with real-time schedules in many cities
- Offline maps available for 100+ countries — download entire regions for use without internet
- Multi-modal trip planning: compare car, bike, transit, taxi, and walking options side by side
- Points of interest search with detailed place information
- Traffic-aware routing with estimated arrival times
- Available on iOS, Android, and as a web app at wego.here.com

## Privacy and Security

HERE Technologies is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates fully under EU jurisdiction and GDPR. The company has a dedicated privacy charter and applies advanced anonymization techniques to all location data. GPS location data is deleted after each navigation session ends. HERE was GDPR-compliant before the regulation even took effect, having designed its privacy framework around European data protection principles from the start. The company generates revenue from B2B mapping services for automotive and enterprise clients, not from advertising or selling personal data.

- Commuters who rely on public transit and want multi-modal trip planning
- Travelers who need reliable offline maps across many countries
