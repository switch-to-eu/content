Thank you for your initiative in presenting European alternatives to the services of the GAFAM companies. I'm surprised not to find Mailo among the Gmail alternatives!
Could you add it?

Mailo is a French and sovereign email service focused on privacy and personal data protection.
It offers an ethical alternative to major international platforms, with data hosted in Europe and compliance with European regulations.
An email specialist, Mailo also provides a calendar, an address book, cloud storage, and sharing tools.
Mailo operates on a freemium model: a free ad-supported service for standard use, and paid Premium plans (ad-free with extended features) starting at around €1 per month, with offers tailored to individuals, families, associations, local authorities, schools, and professionals.
