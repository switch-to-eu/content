## Description

**Category:** new-service

Hosterra Email is an email platform, hosted entirely in France, in eco-friendly environments (with controlled and monitored water and electricity consumption), powered 100% by renewable energy.

Based on open source products, Hosterra Email is a platform that allows to enjoy personalized, spam-free email accounts, calendars, and contact lists, both from standard email clients and from its integrated webmail service.
