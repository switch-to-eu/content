## Description

**Category:** new-service

PixelUnion is a European, privacy-first photo and video storage platform designed as a sovereign alternative to American tech giants like Google Photos and iCloud. Based in the Netherlands and built on the open-source Immich project, it ensures your digital memories are stored on EU-based servers (specifically Germany and Finland).
