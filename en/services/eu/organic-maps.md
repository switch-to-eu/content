Organic Maps is a free, open-source navigation app that works completely offline. Forked from MAPS.ME in 2021, it was rebuilt from the ground up with a privacy-first philosophy — no ads, no tracking, no data collection whatsoever. The app uses OpenStreetMap data and is developed by a global community of contributors.

## Key Features

- Fully offline maps and turn-by-turn navigation for driving, cycling, and walking
- Hiking and cycling trails with elevation profiles and contour lines
- Bookmark and organize favorite places, import KML/KMZ/GPX tracks
- Built-in search for places, addresses, and points of interest — all offline
- Dark mode, automatic map rotation, and 3D building views
- Lightweight: small app size and low battery usage compared to Google Maps
- Available on iOS and Android, completely free with no in-app purchases

## Privacy and Security

Organic Maps collects absolutely no user data. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers — nothing. The app works entirely offline and never contacts external servers unless you manually trigger a map download. The source code is fully open and auditable on GitHub, so anyone can verify these privacy claims. No account is needed to use the app.

## Ideal For

- Privacy-conscious users who want zero-tracking navigation
- Travelers who need reliable offline maps without roaming data
- Hikers and cyclists looking for trail maps and elevation data
- Anyone who wants a fast, lightweight alternative to Google Maps
