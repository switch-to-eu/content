DeepL is a German AI company based in Cologne, specializing in AI-powered translation and writing assistance. Known for producing translations that often surpass competitors in quality, especially for European languages like German, French, and Dutch.

## Key Features

- AI-powered translation supporting over 100 languages with exceptional accuracy
- DeepL Write: AI writing assistant for improving grammar, tone, and style in multiple languages
- DeepL Agent: autonomous AI agent for automating routine business tasks
- Voice API for real-time speech transcription and translation
- Chrome extension and desktop apps for seamless integration into workflows
- Particularly strong in European languages, outperforming major competitors

## Privacy and Security

DeepL is headquartered in Cologne, Germany, and operates fully under EU jurisdiction and GDPR. Data is processed within EU infrastructure. On paid plans, texts are not stored after processing, ensuring that your content remains private. DeepL does not train on user data from paid plans and offers strong enterprise data protection options.

- Users who frequently work with multiple languages, especially European languages

- Businesses that need GDPR-compliant translation and writing tools
